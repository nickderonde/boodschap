BOODSCHAP! is a simple grocery list you share with the people you live with. What one person adds or checks off, the others see too. Free, no ads and no account. Note: the app's interface is in Dutch.

ONE LIST, TOGETHER
• Share a list with a QR code, or send the code through your chat app.
• Changes reach everyone automatically; the top of the list shows whether everything is up to date.
• Check off items, add quantities and notes, and keep several lists (for example the supermarket and the drugstore).

FAST IN THE STORE
• Items are grouped by aisle automatically, in a logical walking order.
• Suggestions based on what you bought before.
• Checked items move to the bottom; clear them with one tap, and undo if you delete something by accident.
• Works without reception. Your changes sync as soon as you are back online.

PRIVACY
• No account, no tracking, no analytics, no ads.
• Your lists are stored on your phone. Shared lists are end-to-end encrypted and travel through public Nostr relays, which cannot read the content.
• There is no BOODSCHAP! server. We cannot see your lists.
• The camera is only used to scan a QR code.

OPEN SOURCE
The source code is public on GitHub, so anyone can verify what the app does.
